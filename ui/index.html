NIGHT CITY DIRECTIVES // COMMUNITY & ROLEPLAY RULES

ENFORCED STRICTLY
DIR-01

FearRP & Value of Life

Your life is your most precious asset in Night City. You must exhibit natural self-preservation when threatened with deadly weapons or facing superior numbers, complying with reasonable orders.

DIR-02

No Random Deathmatch (RDM) & Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Attacking, killing, or running over any player without a fully developed roleplay narrative and established in-character justification is strictly forbidden.

DIR-03

No Metagaming & Powergaming

Using external information obtained outside the game (Discord, streams) is forbidden. Forcing impossible scenarios on other players without giving them realistic opportunity to respond is prohibited.

DIR-04

New Life Rule (NLR)

Upon being critically wounded and revived by Trauma Team, your character loses memory of the immediate events and hostile encounter leading up to your critical injury.

DIR-05

Cyberpsychosis & Heavy Chrome Roleplay

Interact with Night City with an authentic performance matching your character background and chrome level, respecting medical emergency personnel and ripperdocs.
