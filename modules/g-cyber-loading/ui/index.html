NIGHT CITY DIRECTIVES // ROLEPLAY RULES

ENFORCED STRICTLY
DIR-01

Value of Life (FearRP)

Life in Night City is irreplaceable. Value your character's survival at all times, show fear when confronted with lethal weapons, and comply reasonably when outnumbered.

DIR-02

No Random Deathmatch (No RDM / VDM)

Randomly attacking, killing, or vehicular running down of players without prior roleplay interaction and justified narrative conflict is strictly prohibited.

DIR-03

Metagaming & Powergaming Prohibition

Using external knowledge (Discord, streams) in-character is forbidden. Forcing impossible roleplay actions on others without allowing realistic counterplay is disallowed.

DIR-04

New Life Rule (NLR)

Upon incapacitation and respawn via Trauma Team med-center, your character forgets the immediate confrontation and events that caused fatal flatlining.

DIR-05

Identity Immersion & Cyberpsychosis RP

Stay in character, portray the consequences of your cyberware loadout realistically, and respect emergency medical and law enforcement authorities.
